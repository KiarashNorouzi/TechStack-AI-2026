04 · Study Scope

What is intentionally included and excluded from this stage.

Your Current Path ANN Only

What should you watch?

At this stage, the goal is to understand the architecture and learning mechanism of fully-connected artificial neural networks before moving to implementation or specialized architectures.

  • Required videos: approximately 2 hours and 27 minutes.
  • Optional videos: approximately 19 minutes.
  • Module 2 — Neural Networks Basics: skip it for now because Gradient Descent, Logistic Regression, and the related foundations have already been studied.
  • Ian Goodfellow Interview: not required for this ANN learning path.
  • Quizzes and Programming Assignments: not required in this first video-only stage.
Learning Order

Recommended sequence

First understand one hidden layer completely, including activation functions, forward propagation, gradient descent, and backpropagation. Then extend the same concepts to L-layer deep neural networks.
